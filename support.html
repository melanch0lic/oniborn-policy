1. There is no sound

  • On iPhone and iPad, the game follows the Ring/Silent switch, like most games: in silent mode it plays no sound. Turn silent mode off and raise the volume.
  • Check the volumes in the game: Settings → Sound → Music and Effects.
  • If music from another app (a music or podcast app) is playing, it keeps playing and the game's music stays quiet; the game's effects still sound. Stop the other app to hear the game's music.
  • After a call, an alarm or Siri, the game's music comes back by itself. If it does not, leave the game and return to it, and write to us.

2. Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. There is no cloud save, so progress does not move between devices, and the developer has no copy of it and cannot restore it. Uninstalling the game deletes its progress, unless your device's own backup (Google backup on Android, iCloud or a computer backup on iPhone and iPad) restores it. Updating the game keeps your progress.

3. Can I stop a run and finish it later?

Yes. Pause the game and choose Save & exit. In the main menu, Continue run brings you back to where you left. The game also saves the run by itself as you play (at every pause, choice and chest, and every 45 seconds of a fight), so if it is closed in the middle of a fight, Continue run brings you back to its last save.

4. Do I need an internet connection?

No. The whole game plays offline. A connection is used only by the optional Hall of Legends and to tell you when a new version is available.

5. Hall of Legends: name, leaving, a lost place

  • Joining is optional: choose a name, and your best runs are ranked among every player's. Your name is shown to every player, so do not use your real name.
  • Changing your name: Settings → Hall of Legends → Your name.
  • Leaving: Settings → Hall of Legends → Leave the Hall of Legends. Your name and records are removed from the leaderboard for good.
  • "The Hall of Legends cannot be reached": check the connection and try again. Runs finished offline are sent later.
  • "Your place in the Hall was lost": your place is kept on your device only, so reinstalling the game or clearing its data loses it. Join again with a new name. To have your old name and records removed, write to us with that name.

6. Moving the buttons and the movement area

Settings → Fight controls: place the signature skill, the relic's call and the movement area where your thumbs find them, for either hand.

7. Changing the language

Settings → Language. By default the game follows your device's language.

8. Deleting my data

Uninstalling the game deletes everything it keeps on your device. To remove your name and records from the Hall of Legends, leave it first (see question 5), or write to us. Everything the game handles is described in the Privacy Policy.
