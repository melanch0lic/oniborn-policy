1. Introduction

ONIBORN is a single-player roguelite game for Android: you fight waves of demons and bosses as a samurai, unlock characters and maps, and choose upgrades and run modifiers. The game is played offline.

This policy explains what information ONIBORN handles, where it is kept and what control you have over it. It applies to the ONIBORN app for Android distributed through Google Play. ONIBORN is made by an independent developer, the developer named on ONIBORN's Google Play page, referred to here as "we" or "the developer".

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or store any personal information through ONIBORN. The app does not send your progress, your settings or any information about you or your device to the developer or to anyone else.

Information stored only on your device

To work as a game, ONIBORN keeps the following on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Game progress: unlocked characters, maps, relics and run modifiers, character mastery, achievements and the dates they were unlocked.
  • Statistics and records: numbers of runs, victories and defeated enemies, play time, best scores and times, and a history of recent runs.
  • Settings: sound and music on or off and their volumes, the control layout, your last chosen character, map, mode and modifiers, and which tutorial tips you have already seen.
  • A local diagnostic journal: a short list of recent in-game moments (for example, that a night began or a level was reached), the technical details of the last unexpected error, and a marker that shows whether the game closed in the middle of a fight. It helps the game recover safely and contains no personal information. It is never sent anywhere.

Information we do not collect

ONIBORN has no account system and does not ask for your name, email address or any other contact details. It does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files, and it does not request any of the Android permissions that would allow this. It does not use advertising identifiers or other device identifiers.

If you contact us

If you write to us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you.

3. How information is used

The information stored on your device is used by the game itself, on your device, only to:

  • save and load your progress, records and achievements;
  • remember your settings and choices between sessions;
  • show your statistics and run history;
  • recover safely after an unexpected error or an interrupted session.

We do not use any information for advertising, profiling or analytics.

4. Local storage and game progress

Your progress is saved in a file in ONIBORN's private storage on your device. There is no online save and no cloud synchronization run by the developer, so we have no copy of your progress and cannot restore it.

  • Uninstalling ONIBORN deletes its saved data from the device.
  • Clearing the app's storage (in your device's settings, usually Settings → Apps → ONIBORN → Storage → Clear storage) deletes all progress and settings. This cannot be undone.

Android device backup. If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include ONIBORN's saved data in your device backup and may restore it when you reinstall the game or set up a new device. That backup is created and stored by Google under your Google account and your device settings; the developer has no access to it. You can turn device backup on or off in your device's settings. See the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Third-party services

ONIBORN does not include third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, account, payment or social services. The software libraries the game is built with (for graphics, sound and saving files) work entirely on your device and do not send data anywhere.

Google Play

You download and update ONIBORN through Google Play, which is operated by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy. As part of that service, Google provides developers with statistics in the Google Play Console, such as install counts and ratings. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google on your device, Google may also give the developer reports about crashes and freezes. These reports are collected by Google, not by ONIBORN. They show technical details such as the device model, Android version and the error, and do not show the developer your name or contact details.

6. Data sharing and disclosure

ONIBORN does not transmit your information to the developer or to other parties, so we have no personal information from the game to share, sell or disclose. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for advertising.

If you contact us by email, we do not share your message or address with anyone, except where the law requires us to.

7. Data retention and deletion

The data stored on your device stays there until you delete it by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage, as described in section 4. We keep no game data on servers, and since there are no accounts, there is no account to delete.

Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to handle your request. You can ask us to delete your correspondence at any time.

Data held by Google, such as Google Play statistics or a device backup, is kept and deleted according to Google's own policies and your Google account settings.

8. Security

ONIBORN keeps its data in the app's private storage, which Android separates from other apps. Because the game does not send your data over the internet, it is not exposed in transit or on our servers. Still, no method of storage is completely secure: anyone with access to your unlocked device or to your device backups may be able to access the game's data.

9. Children's privacy

ONIBORN is a fantasy action game with stylized combat against demons. Its age rating is shown on its Google Play page.

ONIBORN does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International data transfers

ONIBORN does not transfer your information to other countries, since it does not send it anywhere. Google may process information related to Google Play and device backups in other countries, as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

11. Your choices and rights

You stay in control of ONIBORN's data: it is on your device, and you can delete it at any time. Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the GDPR may give you rights to access, correct or delete personal information about you. Since we do not hold personal information from the game, there is usually nothing for us to provide or delete, but you can always write to us with a question or request, including about any email you have sent us.

12. Changes to this policy

If ONIBORN's data practices change, for example if a future version adds a service that processes information, we will update this policy before or when that version is released. The current version is always published on this page, and the date at the top shows when it was last updated.
