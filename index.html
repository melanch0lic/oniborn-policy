ONIBORN

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

In short

  • ONIBORN is played offline. Your progress, settings and statistics stay on your device.
  • The Hall of Legends, the game's online leaderboard, is optional. Only if you join it by choosing a nickname does the game create an anonymous account and send your nickname and run results to the leaderboard server.
  • You can leave the Hall of Legends at any time in the game's settings, which deletes your nickname and records from the server.
  • The game also checks online whether a new version is available. That check sends nothing about you or your progress.
  • There are no ads, analytics, tracking, crash reporting services or in-app purchases.

1. Introduction

ONIBORN is a single-player roguelite game: you fight waves of demons and bosses as a samurai, unlock characters and maps, and choose upgrades and run modifiers. The game is played offline; an internet connection is used only for the optional online leaderboard and for checking whether an update is available.

This policy explains what information ONIBORN handles, where it is kept and what control you have over it. It applies to the ONIBORN apps for Android, distributed through Google Play, and for iPhone and iPad, distributed through the App Store. ONIBORN is made by an independent developer, the developer named on ONIBORN's store pages, referred to here as "we" or "the developer".

2. Information we collect

ONIBORN does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details, and it has no sign-up with an email or a social account. Apart from the optional leaderboard described below, the app does not send your progress, your settings or information about you to the developer or to anyone else.

Information stored only on your device

To work as a game, ONIBORN keeps the following on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Game progress: unlocked characters, maps, relics and run modifiers, character mastery, achievements and the dates they were unlocked, and a run you saved to continue later.
  • Statistics and records: numbers of runs, victories and defeated enemies, play time, best scores and times, and a history of recent runs.
  • Settings: sound and music on or off and their volumes, the language, the control layout, your last chosen character, map, mode and modifiers, which tutorial tips you have already seen, and your choices about update notices.
  • A local diagnostic journal: a short list of recent in-game moments (for example, that a night began or a level was reached), the technical details of the last unexpected error, and a marker that shows whether the game closed in the middle of a fight. It helps the game recover safely and contains no personal information. It is never sent anywhere.
  • If you joined the Hall of Legends: your nickname, the sign-in session of your anonymous leaderboard account, and the results waiting to be sent while you are offline.

Hall of Legends (optional online leaderboard)

The Hall of Legends ranks players' best results for each character. You can look at the rankings without joining. You join only by choosing a nickname in the game. When you join, the game creates an anonymous account for your installation of the game on the leaderboard server: a random identifier, not linked to your name, email address, phone number or store account. From then on the server stores:

  • your anonymous account identifier;
  • your nickname (3 to 16 characters, chosen by you);
  • the results of your finished runs: an identifier of the run, the character and stance played, the score, the maps cleared, the endless cycles, the time survived, the enemies and bosses defeated, the nights played and won, the evolutions reached, the run modifiers and the score multiplier chosen, the game version, and when the result was achieved and sent.

Your nickname and your best results are public: every player of ONIBORN can see them in the Hall of Legends. Do not use your real name or other personal information as a nickname.

Like any internet service, the leaderboard server receives the IP address of your device and technical details of each request (such as the time) when the game connects to it. They are used to deliver the service and to protect it against abuse, such as limiting how many accounts can be created from one address.

Update check

When the game starts, and from time to time when you come back to it, it downloads a small public file that lists the latest versions of ONIBORN, to tell you when a new version is available. The request contains no information about you, your device or your progress. As with any web request, the server that hosts the file receives your device's IP address and technical details of the request. On Android, the game can also ask Google Play whether an update is available and install it through Google Play.

What ONIBORN does not collect

ONIBORN does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files, and it does not request permissions that would allow this. It does not use advertising identifiers and does not track you across other apps or websites.

If you contact us

If you write to us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you.

3. How information is used

The information stored on your device is used by the game itself, on your device, only to:

  • save and load your progress, records and achievements;
  • remember your settings and choices between sessions;
  • show your statistics and run history;
  • recover safely after an unexpected error or an interrupted session.

The information sent to the Hall of Legends is used only to rank results, to show the rankings, your nickname and your records to players, and to check that results are plausible. The update check is used only to tell you about new versions.

We do not use any information for advertising, profiling, analytics or tracking, and we do not sell it.

4. Local storage and game progress

Your progress is saved in ONIBORN's private storage on your device. There is no online save and no cloud synchronization run by the developer, so we have no copy of your progress and cannot restore it. The Hall of Legends keeps only the leaderboard information listed in section 2, not your saved game.

  • Uninstalling ONIBORN deletes its saved data from the device.
  • On Android, clearing the app's storage (in your device's settings, usually Settings → Apps → ONIBORN → Storage → Clear storage) deletes all progress and settings. This cannot be undone.

Device backups. If backup is turned on for your device, the system may include ONIBORN's saved data in your device backup and may restore it when you reinstall the game or set up a new device: on Android in your Google account (see the Google Privacy Policy), on iPhone and iPad in iCloud or a backup on your computer (see the Apple Privacy Policy). Those backups are created and stored under your account and your device settings; the developer has no access to them. You can turn device backup on or off in your device's settings.

5. Third-party services

ONIBORN does not include third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, payment or social services. The software libraries the game is built with for graphics, sound and saving files work entirely on your device.

Supabase

The Hall of Legends runs on Supabase, which hosts the leaderboard's database and its anonymous accounts on our behalf and processes the information described in section 2 to provide that service. See the Supabase Privacy Policy.

GitHub Pages

The file with the latest versions, and this policy, are hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Google Play and the App Store

You download and update ONIBORN through Google Play, operated by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy, or through the App Store, operated by Apple and governed by the Apple Privacy Policy. As part of those services, the stores provide developers with statistics such as install counts and ratings. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google or Apple on your device, they may also give the developer reports about crashes and freezes. These reports are collected by Google or Apple, not by ONIBORN. They show technical details such as the device model, the system version and the error, and do not show the developer your name or contact details.

Links

The game's settings can open web pages in your browser, such as this policy, the update page of the store and, on Android, a page where you can support the developer. Those websites are not part of the game and have their own privacy policies.

6. Data sharing and disclosure

Your nickname and your best results in the Hall of Legends are shown publicly to other players, as described in section 2. Apart from that, the leaderboard information is processed only by Supabase on our behalf, as described in section 5. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for advertising.

We may disclose information where the law requires us to. If you contact us by email, we do not share your message or address with anyone, except where the law requires us to.

7. Data retention and deletion

The data stored on your device stays there until you delete it by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage, as described in section 4.

Hall of Legends. Your nickname and results are kept on the leaderboard server for as long as you stay in the Hall of Legends. To delete them, open the game's settings and choose to leave the Hall of Legends (an internet connection is needed): your nickname, your records and the results you sent are deleted from the server and the game signs out of your anonymous account. The anonymous account itself is a random identifier with no information left linked to it, and such accounts are removed from time to time. If you uninstall the game without leaving, the game can no longer reach your account: write to us with your nickname, and we will delete your nickname and results.

Information about update checks and leaderboard requests that the hosting providers keep, such as IP addresses in server logs, is kept and deleted according to their policies (see section 5).

Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to handle your request. You can ask us to delete your correspondence at any time.

Data held by Google or Apple, such as store statistics or a device backup, is kept and deleted according to their own policies and your account settings.

8. Security

ONIBORN keeps its data in the app's private storage, which the operating system separates from other apps. Connections to the leaderboard server and to the update file use encrypted HTTPS. On the leaderboard server, each player can change only their own nickname and records. Still, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure: anyone with access to your unlocked device or to your device backups may be able to access the game's data.

9. Children's privacy

ONIBORN is a fantasy action game with stylized combat against demons. Its age rating is shown on its store pages.

ONIBORN does not ask anyone, including children, for their name or contact details. The only information a player chooses to share is the nickname and the results sent to the optional Hall of Legends, which can be deleted at any time as described in section 7. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has shared personal information through a nickname or sent it to us by email, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International data transfers

The leaderboard server and the hosting of the update file may be located outside your country, so the information described in section 2 may be processed in other countries. Google and Apple may process information related to their stores and device backups in other countries, as described in their privacy policies.

11. Your choices and rights

You stay in control of ONIBORN's data. Playing offline, and without joining the Hall of Legends, sends nothing about you. You can change your nickname or leave the Hall of Legends in the game's settings at any time, and you can delete the data on your device at any time.

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the GDPR may give you rights to access, correct or delete personal information about you, or to object to its processing. You can exercise them in the game as described above, or write to us with your nickname and your request.

12. Changes to this policy

If ONIBORN's data practices change, for example if a future version adds a service that processes information, we will update this policy before or when that version is released. The current version is always published on this page, and the date at the top shows when it was last updated.
